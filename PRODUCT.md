# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
COP31 delegates, ministry officials, partner organisations (UNDP, UNFCCC, WFP), researchers and press
checking the Qatar Pavilion programme before and during the conference, 9–20 November 2026, Antalya.
Mostly on phones between sessions, occasionally on a laptop when planning the week. They want the
side-event dates, times and speakers fast, and a credible first impression of Qatar's climate work.

## Product Purpose
One scrolling landing page that is the digital gateway to the Qatar Pavilion at COP31: introduce the
Pavilion, present the five side events interactively, surface key MECC reports, sketch Qatar's climate
action, introduce MECC and hand off to mecc.gov.qa. It does not replicate the MECC site. Success is a
visitor finding a session in seconds and leaving with the impression of a serious, well-run pavilion.

## Brand Personality
Stately, warm, assured. The visual reference is the official COP31 Qatar Pavilion Side-Events
Brochure: Qatar maroon and gold, layered sweeping curves in maroon / tan / gold, a high-contrast
serif set in clearly different weights (heavy display titles, light body), full-bleed photography of
Qatar. Rich and dimensional, not pale or airy.

## Anti-references
- Thin, pale, low-contrast "elegant" typography that reads as washed out on screen.
- Flat single-colour sections with a hairline divider; the brochure's identity is the layered sweep.
- Generic government-portal or SaaS templates; icon-card grids; glassmorphism.
- Anything that looks like the MECC corporate site rather than an event pavilion.

## Design Principles
- Match the brochure, then translate for screen: same colours, same layering, same typographic weight
  contrast, re-scaled for reading distance and WCAG.
- Contrast is the brand: bold titles against light body, gold against maroon, warm ivory against deep
  maroon. Never let a section go flat.
- The programme is the product; every other section exists to frame it.
- Real assets only: named officials, real photography, official marks. No AI-generated people.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA for all text against its real ground. Full keyboard programme tabs, visible focus,
reduced-motion honoured everywhere, 44px touch targets on mobile, noscript fallback.
